How to interpret these numbers

The scores come from preserved research checkpoints and are not calibrated probabilities. A low score does not prove a human speaker; a high score does not prove an attack. Short or silent audio may produce no score. Cross-session speaker robustness and representative joint-risk validation remain open, so Voicegaurd deliberately reports an uncertain verdict.
