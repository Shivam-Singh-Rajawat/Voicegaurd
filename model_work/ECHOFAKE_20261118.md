# EchoFake development audit — November 18, 2026

**This is a measured external-domain failure report, not an acceptance pass.**

## Provenance

- Public dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/EchoFake/EchoFake
- Pinned revision: `4d8bd5f8458b55770d9cac6b944c7bd016168d3a`
- Partition: `dev` only, not a held-out independent test

- 48 audio recordings selected using seed 20261118, with 12 per label sampled

- Recorded sound was downloaded to a temporary private directory per trial

## Outcomes

| Official class | Selected | Scored | Abstained | LA synthetic flags | PA replay flags |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| bonafide (human, not physically replayed) | 12 | 10 | 2 | 0 | 7 |

| fake (synthetic, not physically replayed) | 12 | 10 | 2 | 0 | 4 |

| **Total** | **48** | **41** | **7** | **0** | **31** |

The frozen standalone LA detector flagged **0/19 scored synthetic clips**,
and the frozen PA detector flagged **20/21 scored replays**, but PA also
flagged **7/10 scored non-replayed genuine clips**. The 7 abstentions
were valid incomplete-window detections; no audio downloads/decodes failed.
These values are observed sample counts, not deployment operating rates.

This is evidence of severe domain transfer and an unsuitable uncalibrated
security rule on this scope. Increasing the threshold cannot retroactively
turn these observations into a pass. Do not tune thresholds on EchoFake
evaluation files, and do not pass off exploratory dev selection as heldout
assessment.

## Impact on the two open research gates

**Speaker robustness gate: OPEN**. The dataset exposes a
`source_speaker_id` but does not supply verified enrollment/test
capture-session IDs and target/non-target identity claims per paired trial.
Speaker robustness cannot be measured with this intake. The prior
independent HI-MIA and CN-Celeb1 experiments showed high false nonmatch
rates. Repeating those tests or relabeling source IDs would not improve
the model.

**Calibrated joint risk gate: OPEN**. The four class labels establish
origin/presentation relationships for these recordings and enable a
valuable LA+PA **descriptive** response audit. They do not establish
genuine target, genuine impostor and valid cross-session enrolled claims
for the *same* attacks. Thus they cannot validate calibrated LA/PA/ECAPA
joint risk, its accepted 10%/1%/5%/5%/10% error/abstention limits or the
required speaker/session-aware confidence bounds.

## User-facing labels

Version 1 now displays a mandatory **model prediction label**:
Human / AI-generated / Unknown. The labels are generated at the frozen
diagnostic LA cutoff and explicitly marked provisional; the overall
security verdict stays **Uncertain**.

**Important failure disclosure:** At this frozen cutoff, all 19 scored
synthetic EchoFake development clips would receive the model prediction
"Human". A low LA score, or this provisional label, must never be
presented as proof the voice is genuine. The UI and JSON include a
warning about this observed limitation.

## Next scientifically valid route

For gate 8, obtain actual distinct-session genuine and impostor speaker
trials and compare a prespecified new speaker embedding model and enrollment
strategy against the frozen baseline without retuning on the test data.
For gate 9, collect source-linked human, synthetic and real replay clips
with actual/claimed speaker, authorized enrollment, matched capture context,
speaker-disjoint calibration and held-out assessment. Fit and freeze an
operating policy using calibration only and check the published bounds.
Do not claim 9/9 until both independent outcomes meet approved limits.
